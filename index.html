2 · Position sizing

Adjust to your own account. Quantities update live.
Capital per stock = capital ÷ number of stocks. Quantity = floor(capital per stock ÷ price). Weights blend the three factor ranks exactly as the workbook does (default Mom 2, Value 1, Profitability 1).

3 · Review the selection

Cut any name whose chart looks wrong — the next-best stock fills in automatically and quantities re-size. Click a column header to sort.

4 · Build the basket & save the quarter

Generate the IB BasketTrader file by diffing against your previous portfolio, grab the chart-review import string, and archive everything.
Uninvested cash = the cash balance sitting in your IB account right now (not in any stock). Enter it in your account's base currency. Drop your IB statement into step 4 and the tool will detect the currency and fill this for you.
